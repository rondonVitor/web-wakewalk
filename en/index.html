Questions we hear a lot

Do I have to pay?expand_more

No. The free plan never expires, has no ads and includes six missions, up to 2 alarms, streaks and sleep sounds.

Do the camera or the microphone upload anything?expand_more

No. Push-ups, squats, dance, photos and the spoken phrase are analyzed on your phone. Nothing is sent to servers.

What if the mission doesn't work right then?expand_more

The alarm never gets stuck. If the sky photo doesn't pass, for example, you pick another mission to turn it off.

How do I cancel the Premium trial?expand_more

In the App Store or Google Play: Manage, then Cancel subscription. The app reminds you 2 days before the first charge.

Does the sound keep playing during the mission?expand_more

You choose for each alarm: normal volume, low or silent. On silent, the alarm rings again if you stop halfway through the mission.
